## Publication boundaries

Do not upload `.data/`, runtime binaries, downloaded models, raw credentials or unrelated project logs. Review all evidence files for machine-specific paths before public release. The local URL is not accessible to judges on another machine: use a video walkthrough and repository for submission unless a separate appropriate demo is prepared.
